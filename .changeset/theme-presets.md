Add four themes: Porcelain (cold precision, teal accent), Tobacco (warm pampas cream, terracotta), Marigold (loud poster-print signals, ink-black shadows), Eucalyptus (enterprise titanium blue-gray). Each transforms neutrals, signal colors, and shadow geometry. New `--theme` CLI flag. Interactive demo at `/themes`.
